      Notifications

      Get notified about new messages when TealTalk is closed.

      On iPhone and iPad, add TealTalk to your Home Screen first. Apple only delivers web notifications to apps opened from the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later).

      1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
      2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
      3. Open TealTalk from your Home Screen and come back here.

      Install

      Install TealTalk for a full-screen app with its own icon.

      1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
      2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
      3. Tap Add. TealTalk now opens like any other app.

      About

      TealTalk has no ads, no trackers and no analytics. It loads nothing from anyone else's servers. Your bubbles are teal and everyone else's are grey, on every phone.
